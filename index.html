Fresno County Disc Golf: Privacy Policy

This app shows club members upcoming events, course information, course closures and advisories, and club announcements. It collects no personal information from members.

Effective date: November 18, 2026.

Who publishes this app

The Fresno County Disc Golf app is published by Dave Curry. For any privacy question, write to 3531391@gmail.com.

Members

Members do not create accounts or sign in. The app does not ask for, collect or store your name, email address, phone number, location or any other personal information.

Club staff

A small number of club staff sign in with an email address and password so they can post events, closures, advisories and announcements. The staff email address is used only for signing in and for managing who is allowed to post. It is not shown to members, sold or shared for advertising.

Information the app shows

Events, courses, announcements and club contacts shown in the app are supplied by the club. A contact's name and role appear only with that person's consent.

Notifications

The app does not currently send push notifications. If that changes, this policy will be updated first, and notifications will always be optional.

Services we use

The app stores club information and staff sign-in details using Google Firebase. Google's own privacy policy applies to its services.

Links to other sites

Course pages link to UDisc, and some events link to outside registration pages. Those sites have their own privacy policies, and this policy does not cover them.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted in the app's Settings under Privacy Policy, with a new effective date.
